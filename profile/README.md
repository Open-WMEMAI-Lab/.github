Open World Models for EMbodied Artificial Intelligence Lab (**Open × WMEMAI Lab**) is my research initiative centered on a fundamental question: **How can intelligent agents autonomously acquire, organize, and continually refine an understanding of the world, and use that knowledge to reason, plan, and act reliably in unfamiliar environments?**

My research interests lie in self-supervised predictive learning, structured world models, and the discovery of causal and compositional abstractions from high-dimensional, continuous, and noisy experience. I seek to understand how agents can learn transferable representations and executable theories of their environments, predict the consequences of actions, and generalize beyond previously encountered situations.

My long-term research ambition is to advance the foundations of **human- and animal-like general intelligence** through **lifelong, open-ended, self-improving embodied agents**. I aim to investigate systems that progressively acquire and retain world knowledge, autonomously explore and learn from experience, identify limitations in their own models, and revise their representations, theories, and plans as new evidence becomes available.

Central to this vision is the development of **interpretable, controllable, and aligned intelligence**, in which autonomous learning and adaptation are grounded in principled mathematical formulations, explicit safety constraints, uncertainty-aware reasoning, and verifiable guarantees wherever achievable.

I am also interested in **AI scientists** capable of autonomously forming mechanistic hypotheses, designing informative experiments, testing competing explanations, and refining scientific models through evidence-driven discovery.

### Research Interests

- **Self-Supervised Predictive World Models** — Joint-embedding predictive architectures, energy-based learning, structured representations, and action-conditioned prediction in abstract latent spaces.
- **Causal, Object-Centric, and Compositional World Understanding** — Discovering reusable concepts, causal structure, executable world theories, and abstractions that support systematic generalization.
- **Reasoning and Model-Based Planning** — Hierarchical reasoning, predicting action consequences, planning with learned world models, and compositional transfer of knowledge and skills.
- **Embodied and Physical Intelligence** — Continual sensorimotor learning, closed-loop planning and control, and generalization across unfamiliar physical environments and robotic tasks.
- **Lifelong, Open-Ended, and Safe Self-Improving Intelligence** — Persistent memory, autonomous exploration, continual knowledge acquisition and revision, detection of model inadequacy, interpretability, alignment, and mathematically grounded safety and reliability.
- **AI Scientists and World-Theory Discovery** — Autonomous hypothesis generation, causal and mechanistic reasoning, experimental design, and evidence-driven refinement of scientific models.

### Research and Development

My aim is to contribute principled methods and credible scientific evidence toward **lifelong, self-improving, safe, and reliable machine intelligence**—intelligent systems capable of progressively understanding how the world works and using that understanding to reason, discover, plan, and act.
